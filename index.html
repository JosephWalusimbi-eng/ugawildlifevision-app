UgaWildlifeVision

Take a photo of an animal and the app will name the species.

Tip: fill the frame with the animal. Zoom in if it is far away.

Early test version. On 47 real phone photos from UWEC it was right about 1 time in 5 (zebra and tiger worked well; chimpanzee, birds, lion and buffalo did not). Treat answers as a guess.

      About this app and its limits
      • The model runs on your phone. Your photos are not uploaded anywhere.
      • It knows 26 kinds of animal. Anything else should come out as "Not one of the species I know", but it can still be wrong.
      • Lion, buffalo, cheetah, kudu and hyena had very few training photos. Treat those answers with caution.
      • It names one animal per photo. With several animals, crop or zoom to one.
      • Local-language names are shown only after a native speaker has checked them.
      • A student capstone project (Part 2). Not for safety-critical decisions.
