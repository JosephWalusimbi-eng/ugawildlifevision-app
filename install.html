UgaWildVision

Point your phone at an animal and find out what it is.
Works offline. Your photos stay on your phone.

https://josephwalusimbi-eng.github.io/ugawildlifevision-app/

Open the app

1. Scan

  1. Open your phone's camera and point it at the QR code, then tap the link that appears.

2. Add to your home screen

  1. Android (Chrome): tap the menu (⋮) and choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
  2. iPhone (Safari): tap Share (the square with an arrow) and choose Add to Home Screen.

3. Use it anywhere

  1. Open it once while you have a connection. After that it works without internet.
  2. Take a close, clear photo of one animal. Choose a language at the top to see and hear its name.

Names in local and visitor languages are machine-generated or common names and are still being checked by native speakers.
